# Telenotify trademark policy

The Telenotify **code** is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The Telenotify **name, logo and "Verified business" badge** are not covered by that license. They identify the official project and the hosted service run by its maintainer (Samuel Yeshambel), so people can tell the real thing from a copy.

This policy explains what you can and can't do with them. It's short on purpose; if you're unsure, ask in an issue.

## You can, without asking

- Say that your product **uses**, **works with** or **is built on** Telenotify ("Powered by Telenotify", "Notifications via Telenotify").
- Write about Telenotify: blog posts, tutorials, videos, courses, reviews.
- Run the code for yourself or your organization, unmodified or modified, as the AGPL allows.
- Use the name when contributing to this repository, or in a fork you keep for sending pull requests back.

## You need written permission to

- Call your own hosted service, product or company **Telenotify**, or a confusingly similar name (Telenotifi, TeleNotify Pro, Telenotify Ethiopia…).
- Use the Telenotify logo for anything other than linking to the official project.
- Offer a modified version publicly **under the Telenotify name**. If you run a changed copy for others, give it a different name and say it is based on Telenotify (and share your changes, as the AGPL requires).
- Use a domain name, Telegram bot username or social account that suggests it's official (for example `telenotify-pay.com` or `@telenotify_support_bot`).

## You may never

- Show the **"Verified business"** badge, or say a business is "verified by Telenotify", unless the official service verified it. Only the official service can grant the badge; anyone can check it at the `/check` page of the official site. Faking it is misleading to the people the badge protects.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests the maintainer endorses you when they don't.

## Why

Customers trust a message more when they know where it comes from. If anyone could call their service Telenotify or show the badge, that trust would mean nothing, for businesses and for their customers. This policy only protects the name; the code stays free.
